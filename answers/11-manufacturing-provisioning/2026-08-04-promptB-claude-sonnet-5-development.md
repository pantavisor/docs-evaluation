## What the work instruction and answers came out to

Bare board → shippable, from the docs alone: (1) obtain a pre-built image from `pantavisor.io/downloads` or build one; (2) put the board in USB download mode and run the board's `pv-flash-bundle`/`flash.sh` (NXP/Toradex/Variscite families) or write the `.wic` with `pvflasher` (SD-card boards); (3) power on, watch first boot over serial; (4) optionally, at claim time, read `/pv/device-id` and `/pv/challenge` from the debug shell and enter them at hub.pantacor.com to claim the unit. Per-unit uniqueness (Q1), internet requirement (Q2), and time-per-unit (Q4) could not be answered with confidence — flagged S1 above. Claiming (Q3) *does* reach a real runnable command (`cat /pv/device-id`, `cat /pv/challenge` from the serial debug shell, documented on the meta-pantavisor operate page) — the trail does not dead-end. Rework (Q5) and shipping test (Q6) are only partially answered, per the S1/S3 rows above.
